How ReadWell works

One parent account can look after several children. Each child gets their own space.

  1. Create a parent account

    Sign up with your name, email and a password. We ask for nothing else.

  2. Add your children

    Enter a name and grade level for each child. ReadWell makes a username and PIN for them.

  3. Your child signs in

    They use their username and PIN to reach their own dashboard, separate from everyone else's.

Built to be easy to read and easy to use

Some students find busy screens hard to follow. ReadWell keeps every page plain and predictable.

  • Readable text Large type in the student area, strong contrast and generous spacing.
  • Works with a keyboard Every control can be reached and used without a mouse, with a clear focus outline.
  • Clear next steps Each page has one main action, and messages say what went wrong and how to fix it.
  • Quiet design No flashing, no autoplay and no clutter.
